# Investor Sidecar — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 27 July 2026_

Investor Sidecar is a Chrome extension that analyzes real-estate investment deals — buy and
hold, fix and flip, and BRRRR — for listings you choose to save while browsing Redfin and
Zillow.

## Short version

Investor Sidecar does not collect, transmit, or sell any of your data. Everything it stores
stays in your own browser. There are no accounts, no analytics, no third-party
services, and the extension contacts no server of any kind.

## What is stored, and where

When you click **Analyze** on a listing, the extension reads the details already visible on
that page — address, price, beds, baths, square footage, HOA, listing ID, and the listing's
coordinates — and saves them with your calculator settings (down payment, interest rate,
tax and insurance rates, and any per-property overrides you enter).

The extension also keeps a short undo history — the previous state of the last few
properties you changed or deleted, so a deletion can be reversed. It holds at most twenty
entries and is overwritten as you keep working.

All of it is written to `chrome.storage.local`, which is storage on your own computer. It is
not Chrome Sync storage, so it is not copied to your Google account or to your other
devices. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any server.

You can delete a saved property at any time from the side panel, and removing the extension
deletes everything it stored, including the undo history.

## Permissions and why they exist

- **Redfin and Zillow site access** — to place the Analyze button on listings and read the
  listing details from the page. The extension runs on no other websites.
- **Storage** — to save your properties and settings on your computer.
- **Side panel** — to show the calculator alongside the page.

## Data sold or shared

None. Investor Sidecar does not sell, share, or transfer any user data to third parties, and
does not use any data for advertising, creditworthiness, or lending purposes.

## Changes

If a future version collects anything, this policy and the Chrome Web Store data disclosures
will be updated before that version ships.
